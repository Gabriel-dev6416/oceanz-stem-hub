Route 1

Learn a skill and teach to earn

For people trained by the OCEANZ Teacher Academy. Training runs on WhatsApp and Telegram. After training, graduates complete the recruitment form, and our team reviews each one before sending an invitation to teach.

Route 2

Already skilled? Apply to teach

For experienced teachers. You apply online with your qualifications, a 2 to 3 minute introduction video and two referees, then have an interview with a 15-minute demonstration lesson. Applicants outside Nigeria are welcome.

Safeguarding statement

OCEANZ STEM HUB puts the safety of children first. Every teacher is verified (identity, qualifications, references and TRCN where it applies), interviewed or trained by our Academy, and agrees to our child-safeguarding code of conduct before teaching.

Teachers never message learners under 18 privately, and online one-on-one lessons with children are recorded.
